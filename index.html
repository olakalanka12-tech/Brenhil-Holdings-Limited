About us

Brenphil Holdings Limited started as a small business in 2017 and was incorporated as a private limited company on 18 May 2023 under the Companies Act, 2015. We offer supply solutions tailored to a range of industries.

Our management team is made up of professionals in business and marketing. We practise transparency and integrity in financial management and follow all compliance and management regulations.
